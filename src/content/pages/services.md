## Tailored Cigar Services in the Flathead Valley

Beyond walk-in retail sales, we curate specialized services for collectors, outdoor adventurers, wedding parties, and celebratory milestones throughout Northwestern Montana.

### 1. Rare Box Allocations & Special Orders
Looking for hard-to-find vitolas or full factory boxes? We partner directly with premium distributors to secure rare allocations and collector boxes for enthusiasts in the Flathead Valley.

- **Priority Allocations**: First access to limited release boxes, Arturo Fuente OpusX, and Davidoff seasonal releases.
- **Factory Box Pricing**: Special box pricing on complete 10, 20, and 25-count boxes.
- **Precision Humidor Storage**: Boxes are held in our walk-in Spanish cedar humidor at 67% relative humidity until pickup.

### 2. Wedding & Private Event Cigar Bars
Add timeless sophistication to your Montana wedding, lakeside gathering, or private ranch party. We provide turnkey cigar packages tailored to your event:

- **Custom Curated Cigar Menus**: Mild, medium, and full-bodied selections matched to your guests' tastes.
- **Cutting & Lighting Accessories**: Premium multi-flame butane torches, precision guillotine and V-cutters, and cedar spills.
- **Personalized Cigar Bands**: Commemorate your wedding date, monogram, or retreat logo on custom printed bands.

### 3. Fast Curbside Pickup & Travel Prep
Heading into Glacier National Park, Flathead Lake, or the ski mountain? Call ahead and our team will have your order freshly cut, checked, and packaged in protective Boveda-humidified travel bags for convenient curbside pickup.

### 4. Retail Humidor & Daily Inventory Selections
We take pride in maintaining Montana's most comprehensive selection of the world's finest hand-made and everyday cigars:
- **Handmade Premium Icons**: Complete lines of Plasencia, Oliva (Serie V & Melanio), Rocky Patel, Romeo y Julieta, My Father Cigars, Acid by Drew Estate, Ashton, Cohiba, Havana Honeys, Macanudo, Montecristo, NUB, Padrón, Perdomo, Punch, Tatiana, Davidoff, La Aroma de Cuba, La Aurora, Tatuaje, and Arturo Fuente.
- **Everyday Classics**: Fresh packs of Backwoods (all natural leaf flavors), Black & Mild (wood & plastic tip), Swisher Sweets, Game natural leaf, and Dutch Masters.

Contact us today to check current box allocations, place a special order, or coordinate cigar packages for your upcoming event.
